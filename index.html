۰۱ From the atelier

A saree is a conversation with the woman wearing it. We weave slowly — yarn dyed in iron, indigo and madder, threads counted by hand. We refuse the loud, the rushed, the printed shortcut. Every drape is signed, dated, and held a season before it leaves the studio.

— Laila Khan, founder

۰۴ Four hands, four weeks A method
  1. 01

    Listen

    A conversation in the studio — heirlooms shown, occasions named, colours sat with.

  2. 02

    Dye

    Yarn cooked in iron, madder root, indigo vat, marigold petal — slowly, in copper.

  3. 03

    Weave

    One loom, one weaver, one piece at a time. Zari pulled by hand, never machined.

  4. 04

    Send

    Folded in muslin, tied with raw cotton, delivered with a hand-written note.
