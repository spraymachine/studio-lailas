۰۵ How a piece is made A method
  1. 01

    Fit

    We begin with measurements and a conversation — occasion, fabric weight, how you like to move.

  2. 02

    Cut

    Fabric selected from trusted mills. Each piece hand-cut against a paper pattern made for your body.

  3. 03

    Stitch

    One tailor per piece. Seams pressed at every stage. Embroidery and finishing done only by hand.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    A final fitting before it leaves the studio. Wrapped in muslin, with a hand-written note.
